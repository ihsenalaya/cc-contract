# RQ1 — existing evidence, immutable

The [published H100 pilot](../environment/state-continuity-pilot-result.md) is
complete: 50 serial runs, 10 healthy and 40 injected; 40 activated, CUDA_SUCCESS
and detected, zero misses, zero alerts among the 10 healthy runs. The independent
audit and private original hashes are linked in its published manifest.

This establishes bounded feasibility for L1/L2/C1/C2, with the stated trusted
instrumentation. It does not establish prevalence, broad sensitivity, a low
population false-positive rate, seven-field hardware observation or superiority.
The 50 runs are neither repeated nor pooled with HDSC v1. Likewise, the historical
140-job campaign remains separate and unchanged. RQ2–RQ4 GPU results do not yet exist.
